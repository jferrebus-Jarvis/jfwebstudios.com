Our Texas Heritage

HONORING THE ART OF CLASSIC BUTCHERY

At JP Prime Cuts TX, butchery isn't just a business—it's a sacred Texas craft. Founded in Houston, our butcher shop was built on a simple commitment: bring back the personal trust, master skill, and uncompromised quality of traditional meat markets.

We source exclusively from family-owned Texas ranches that practice ethical, grain-finished raising. Every subprimal that enters our shop is inspected, aged in our Himalayan salt tile room, and carved by hand with pride.
