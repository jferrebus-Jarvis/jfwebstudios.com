Uncompromising Craftsmanship

The JP Prime Cuts Standard

“Our philosophy is simple: source only the top 1% of American and Japanese beef, honor traditional artisanal butchery, and deliver an unmatched culinary experience to Texas tables.”
— Julian Perez, Founder & Master Butcher

Founded in Houston, Texas, JP Prime Cuts TX was established with a single vision: to elevate butchery from a routine trade to an extraordinary culinary craft. We partner exclusively with sustainable, family-owned ranches in Texas, the Midwest, and Miyazaki Prefecture who share our dedication to ethical animal care, grain feeding, and natural growth.

Inside our state-of-the-art facility, every carcass is evaluated for muscle tone, fat color, and intramuscular marbling. Our flagship dry-aging vault is lined with pure pink Himalayan salt bricks, controlling humidity and temperature to transform primal cuts into rich, deeply fragrant masterpieces.
