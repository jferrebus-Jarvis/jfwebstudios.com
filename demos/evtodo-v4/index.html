Powering the Next Era of Transportation

Founded in 2019 in Houston, Texas, EVTODO has established itself as a leader in commercial EV charging infrastructure. We specialize in EV charging station solutions for commercial fleets, OEM operators, dealerships, federal/state transportation agencies, and shopping/leisure areas.

Whether electrifying municipal transit depots, last-mile delivery fleets, electric school buses, or regional transportation networks, our team provides full end-to-end turnkey engineering backed by certified field technicians and guaranteed uptime SLA contracts.

Turnkey Methodology

Our Installation Process

A proven, timeline-driven deployment framework ensuring project safety, utility grid synchronization, and zero fleet operational disruption.

1
STAGE 01

Site Assessment & Fleet Power Audit

We analyze vehicle route schedules, peak kilowatt load requirements, existing electrical infrastructure, and utility capacity to design the optimal charging layout.

2
STAGE 02

Engineering Design & Utility Permitting

Our engineers craft detailed single-line electrical diagrams, site civil plans, and submit utility interconnect applications to expedite AHJ permitting approvals.

3
STAGE 03

Full Turnkey Civil & Grid Infrastructure

Full turnkey civil work including directional boring, underground conduit installation, concrete pad pouring, switchgear setting, and utility transformer placement.

4
STAGE 04

DC Level 3 Charging Stations Setup

Hardware installation of DC Level 3 Charging Stations (Heliox, EVTech, BorgWarner, ABB, Siemens), cable management setup, and electrical safety validation.

5
STAGE 05

Commissioning & Guaranteed Uptime Onboarding

Full system commissioning, network software onboarding, real-world vehicle charging tests, and activation of our guaranteed uptime programs.
