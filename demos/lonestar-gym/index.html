Who We Are

Built for
Athletes.

Lonestar Athletics is a premier training facility in Katy, TX designed to give young athletes the competitive edge they need. A fair, fun, and positive environment where athletes grow and compete.

Our coaches develop not just better athletes — but better people. Skill, confidence, and a championship mindset are built into every session.

  • Fair, Fun & Positive Environment
  • Develop Skill, Confidence & Mindset
  • Youth to High School Athletes
  • College Prep & Recruiting Support
  • Sport-Specific Coaching
  • Discipline-First Culture

What We Offer

Training Programs

Programs designed for every level — elementary through high school

🏈

Sport Specific Training

Football, basketball, baseball and more. We train the movements your sport demands so you're ready when it matters most.

⚡

Speed & Agility

First-step quickness, lateral speed, and 40-yard dash improvement. Our athletes have hit 11.8 MPH top speed and 0.869s first-5 splits.

💪

Strength & Conditioning

Build the foundation of athleticism. Functional strength, power development, and conditioning that transfers to the field.

🎓

College Athlete Program

Built specifically for athletes ready to take the next step. Custom programming, film prep, and recruiting guidance.

🛡️

Injury Prevention

Movement screening and corrective work to keep athletes healthy all season long. Train hard, stay on the field.

🧠

Mental Performance

Discipline, focus, and a winning mindset are built into every session at Lonestar.
