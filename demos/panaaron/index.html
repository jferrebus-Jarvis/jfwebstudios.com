From Caracas to Houston

Aaron is a Venezuelan artisan baker who blends traditional Latin baking techniques with natural sourdough fermentation — crafting uniquely flavorful, healthy loaves that celebrate his roots from Caracas.

Every loaf is made by hand using premium, fresh, and natural ingredients. No shortcuts. No additives. Just real bread, made with real love.
