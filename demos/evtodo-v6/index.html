Target Sectors

Purpose-Built Infrastructure Across 7 Markets

EVTODO custom designs charging ecosystems tailored to the unique operational demands, power requirements, and schedules of 7 distinct commercial sectors.

Electric School Buses

K-12 & School Districts

Turnkey depot charging solutions for electric school bus fleets. Scheduled overnight power management and Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G) bi-directional capability to support district energy resilience.

Public Transit & Shuttles

Federal & Municipal Agencies

High-power DC fast charging and automated pantographs for municipal bus transit systems. Designed to keep transit fleets operating seamlessly on strict schedule headways.

Shopping & Leisure

Retail, Hotels & Venues

Customer-facing Level 2 and DC Fast Charging stations for shopping malls, lifestyle centers, resorts, and entertainment complexes to increase dwell time and visitor revenue.

Auto Dealerships

OEM Franchises & Service Bays

OEM-compliant high-voltage charging infrastructure for dealership showrooms, prep bays, and customer staging areas. Tailored to support brand electrification mandates.

Trucks & Heavy Cargo

Class 8 Freight & Drayage

Megawatt-scale charging stations engineered for Class 8 electric semi-trucks, port drayage operators, and heavy logistics centers requiring rapid turnaround times.

Delivery Fleets

Last-Mile Logistics

High-density commercial van charging hubs for last-mile delivery providers. Smart load-balancing ensures full fleet readiness every morning without oversubscribing utility grid ties.

Airports & GSE

Aviation & Rental Fleets

Specialized charging for airport Ground Support Equipment (GSE), passenger shuttle vans, rental car turnaround facilities, and long-term traveler parking areas.
