✦ About the Artist

Precision.
Artistry.
Perfection.

Anabel Gómez is an internationally recognized brow artist — featured in Forbes Style & Beauty, Diario de México, and named one of the Most Powerful Women in Latin America at the Women's Summit. based in Orlando, FL — specializing in micropigmentation, brow lamination, and precision waxing that transforms your look with lasting results.

With over 42,000 followers and hundreds of stunning transformations, Anabel has built a reputation for creating brows that are naturally flawless — tailored to your unique face shape and personal style.

Located at Deerfield Shopping Center, her studio is an intimate luxury experience from the moment you walk in.
