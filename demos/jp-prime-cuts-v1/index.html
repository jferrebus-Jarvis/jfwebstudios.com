The JP Prime Heritage

Carving Texas Excellence Since 2018

Founded on the belief that real butchery is an art form, JP Prime Cuts TX bridges the gap between Texas cattle ranching traditions and refined culinary craftsmanship.

Every piece of beef that enters our shop is sourced exclusively from sustainable Texas and Midwest ranches committed to ethical animal husbandry, zero added hormones, and superior genetics.

Our master butchers inspect each primal cut for optimal marbling, color, and cap size before placing it into our custom dry-aging room or meticulously hand-carving it to order.
