Our 4-Step Deployment Process

A predictable, transparent engineering workflow that takes your fleet from concept to fully energized charging infrastructure.

01

Site Assessment & Audit

We inspect site power capacity, evaluate fleet duty cycles, consult with local electric utilities, and deliver a full feasibility report.

02

Engineering & Hardware

Custom electrical engineering designs, utility interconnect plans, and selection of optimal Heliox / EVTech charging hardware.

03

Turnkey Construction

Civil excavation, concrete pads, electrical wiring, transformer installation, charger mounting, and safety inspection.

04

Commissioning & SLA

Final grid energization, cloud software onboarding, staff training, and activation of our 99.9% guaranteed uptime maintenance program.
