Our 5-Step Turnkey Deployment Process

From initial site feasibility to final safety certification, we streamline every step of fleet charging installation.

1

Site Assessment

Comprehensive evaluation of utility transformer capacity, fleet dwell times, depot layout, and peak energy demand.

2

Engineering Design

Custom civil and electrical schematics, selecting optimal Heliox or EVTech DC Level 3 chargers and energy cabinets.

3

Civil Construction

Securing municipal permits, site trenching, conduit installation, concrete pad pouring, and utility service upgrades.

4

Commissioning

High-voltage electrical wiring, charging dispenser mounting, network telematics integration, and load testing.

5

Uptime Enrollment

Staff operator training, network software handoff, and activation of 24/7 preventive & corrective maintenance SLAs.
